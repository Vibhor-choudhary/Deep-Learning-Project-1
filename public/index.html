Research Methodology & Engineering Decisions

1. The Scientific Question

Does a compact CNN retain higher classification fidelity and robustness against image distortions compared to a flattened MLP when training data is strictly limited?

By training on a balanced subset of just 100 samples per class, the experiment tests the inductive bias of spatial convolutions against fully connected architectures.

2. Dataset Attribution

UCI Devanagari Handwritten Character Dataset (DHCD), published under CC BY 4.0.

Contains 92,000 images across 46 classes (36 consonants ka through gya, and 10 Devanagari digits 0 through 9). Evaluated strictly on the official held-out test split of 13,800 images.

3. Confidence-Aware Rejection

In safety-critical OCR, wrong predictions can be worse than no prediction. AksharCoach selects rejection thresholds via validation ROC-accuracy trade-offs (20% for CNN, 50% for MLP). When a test sample falls below this score, it is flagged as Uncertain.
