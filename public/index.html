AksharCoach

Offline recognition of one isolated handwritten Devanagari character or digit. It does not read Hindi words, sentences, or paragraphs.

Neural Engine: Ready (CNN ONNX)

Batch mode — use this for more than one character

Upload a plain image with separately spaced handwritten characters or an alphabet chart. The app automatically segments individual characters and strips solid/dashed table grid lines. Note: The deep learning model is an isolated-character classifier trained on the 46 UCI classes (36 consonants क–ज्ञ and 10 digits ०–९). Hindi vowels like अ, आ, इ... and connected words are outside the 46 classes and will be flagged as Uncertain.

About AksharCoach

Question: Does a compact CNN remain more robust than a flattened-image MLP with limited training data?

The UCI Devanagari Handwritten Character Dataset (CC BY 4.0) supplies 46 classes of 32×32 grayscale images. The CNN retains spatial structure through convolution and pooling; the MLP uses 1,024 flattened pixels. Scores are softmax model scores, not calibrated probabilities.

Source code, full technical report, viva defense guide, and training pipelines are open source on GitHub:
https://github.com/Vibhor-choudhary/Deep-Learning-Project-1
